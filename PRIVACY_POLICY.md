# Privacy Policy for MealScanner

**Last Updated: February 20, 2026**

MealScanner ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by MealScanner.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Information:** When you sign up, we collect your email address and any profile information you provide through Supabase Auth.
- **Meal Data:** We collect photos of food you scan, nutritional data generated, and journal entries to provide the core functionality of the app.
- **Health Data:** With your explicit permission, we access Apple Health data including step count, active energy, basal energy, body mass, height, biological sex, and date of birth.
- **Usage Data:** We collect information about how you interact with the app to improve our services.
- **Security Data:** We collect IP addresses for security purposes, including abuse prevention and rate limiting. This data is not linked to your personal account and is used solely for security monitoring.
- **Purchase Information:** We use RevenueCat to process subscriptions. We do not store your credit card information; it is handled by the Apple App Store.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To provide and maintain our service.
- To personalize your nutrition targets using health and profile data.
- To sync your meal nutritional data back to Apple Health.
- To process your subscriptions and payments.
- To improve our AI models for better nutritional accuracy.
- To communicate with you about updates or support.

## 3. Apple Health Integration
MealScanner integrates with Apple Health (HealthKit) to provide a personalized experience:
- **Read Access:** We read active energy, basal energy, and step count to adjust your daily nutrition goals based on activity. We read body measurements (weight, height) and profile data (biological sex, date of birth) to calculate your metabolic needs.
- **Write Access:** We write meal calories and macronutrients (protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar, sodium) to your Apple Health history to help you maintain a centralized health record.

**Privacy and Security of Health Data:**
- Health data is stored locally on your device or in your iCloud account as managed by Apple Health.
- We do not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or other use-based data mining purposes.
- We do not share HealthKit data with third parties without your explicit consent, and only for the purpose of providing health or fitness services.

## 4. Data Storage and Security
Your data is securely stored using Supabase (PostgreSQL and Storage) and processed through secure API endpoints. We implement industry-standard security measures, access controls, and encryption in transit to protect your data.

## 5. Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services:
- **Supabase:** For authentication and database management.
- **RevenueCat:** For subscription management and payment processing.
- **OpenRouter:** As an AI routing provider for parts of meal analysis and nutrition generation.
- **Google Gemini (via OpenRouter):** For AI-powered nutrition analysis and report generation.
- **OpenAI Whisper:** For speech-to-text transcription when voice meal input is used.

When we send content (for example, meal descriptions, food images, or audio) to these providers, we send only what is necessary to deliver the feature you requested.

## 6. Your Rights
You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app settings or by contacting us.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@mealscanner.app.
